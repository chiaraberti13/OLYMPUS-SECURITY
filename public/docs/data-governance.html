System data retention and privacy policy (DATA-3)

This is the single policy of record for how long Olympus keeps each class of sensitive material, how that material is classified for privacy, and — for evidence — how its lifecycle is made verifiable. It unifies mechanisms that already exist: bounded retention and secure deletion (olympus.core.retention, see retention.md), the signed, hash-chained custody ledger (olympus.minerva.custody), and the transactional store's own pruning (ThemisJobStore.prune). The policy itself lives in olympus.minerva.governance (SYSTEM_POLICY), so it is executable, not only prose.

Data classes

Every sensitive class is registered with a bounded retention rule (age, count or size — never "keep everything"), a privacy classification, how retention is enforced, and whether it is tracked in the verifiable custody chain.

The windows are conservative documented defaults — the mechanism, not the exact number of days, is the contract; an operator tightens them per engagement. Raw scanner output (least summarised, most sensitive) is kept the shortest; signed evidence and finding history the longest.

Evidence historicisation with a verifiable custody chain

Signed engine-run evidence (TOOLS-4.5) is the custody-tracked class. Its lifecycle is recorded in the Minerva custody ledger: each entry anchors the evidence SHA-256, is hash-chained to the previous entry, and — when a key is configured — is Ed25519-signed so a third party can verify that the chain was not truncated or rewritten (SEC-E). governance.verify_custody reuses custody.inspect_ledger with the environment's key to confirm the chain is evidence-anchored and, if signed, valid. Retention never deletes an evidence artefact without that history remaining verifiable in the ledger.

Operating it

# Show the whole policy as JSON.
olympus minerva governance-policy

# Apply a class's retention to a directory (dry-run first).
olympus minerva governance-apply engine-evidence ./.olympus/out --dry-run
olympus minerva governance-apply scanner-output ./.olympus/out

# Verify the custody chain that historicises evidence.
olympus minerva governance-verify-custody ./.olympus/minerva-custody.json

Classes enforced elsewhere refuse a directory pass with a pointer to the right tool: job-records → olympus themis jobs prune; audit-trail → olympus themis retention rotate (log rotation). This keeps one authority per class rather than two mechanisms deleting the same data.

Privacy

contains_identities / contains_targets mark the classes that name people or authorized targets; redact on egress marks the classes that must be redacted before leaving the perimeter (the same discipline the SIEM/CTI connectors already apply). This policy is the reference the AI egress gate (AI-2, ADR-011) and any export path check against.

Scope and limits

Retention here bounds files on disk and store rows; as retention.md states, dependable erasure of data at rest is full-disk/filesystem encryption, and this is defence in depth on top of it, not a replacement. The concrete Postgres backend for job-records is out of the offline perimeter (DATA-2), but the same policy windows apply once it exists.
